Research embedded in the trainer

Room patternRBs gone by pick 20: 8 in 2023, 8 in 2024, 8 in 2025. That is why an elite RB start remains attractive.
QB marketYour room took roughly 2–3 QBs by pick 20, ~6 by pick 40, ~8 by pick 60. The current room model adds soft targets plus hard catch-up floors so elite QBs cannot unrealistically survive into the late rounds.
TE marketUsually 0–1 TE by pick 20, around 3 by pick 40, then a run before pick 60. If Bowers/McBride miss 20/21, 40/41 is the Loveland/Warren pivot.
ConstructionBecause you start 2 FLEX, RB/WR depth is highly usable. After QB + TE are secured, favor RB/WR ceiling over backup QB/TE.
Injury resilienceYour past seasons have been injury-heavy. We cannot predict injuries, so early picks emphasize secure volume; bench picks emphasize players whose roles can jump sharply.
WaiversYou and generaljjj have had high waiver activity partly from injuries, so transaction count is not treated as pure drafting style.
PlayoffsYour postseason runs Weeks 15–17. 2026 bye weeks are tracked by NFL team and used only as a roster-balance tiebreaker, never as a reason to pass a clearly better player.
Late roundsDo not spend early/mid picks on K/DEF. Default plan is to fill them in the final two rounds, unless the board forces something unusual.
Hybrid ranking layerBest Available now uses embedded current PPR market anchors first, then a cached Sleeper player search rank for players we have not manually anchored. Deep players no longer inherit an artificially high old list position.
Champion ProcessRepeat-winner principles are now scored on every pick: market value, positional cliffs, estimated survival to your next turn, roster construction, and asymmetric upside. QB/TE run-chasing and redundant backups are penalized.
League QB scoringThe current scoring model separates rushing-leverage QBs from pocket passers. With 4-point passing TDs and 6-point rushing TDs, Allen/Lamar/Daniels/Hurts-type profiles receive a meaningful boost; Mahomes/Burrow/Herbert-type profiles must win on value rather than generic QB-run pressure.
Current calibrationCurrent anchors include Brian Thomas Jr. and Jaxson Dart, refreshed QB market values, current top D/ST and kicker tiers, and a final-turn DEF+K pairing rule. v6.8 applies the QB starter-quality order before the forced-QB turn too, so ADP freshness or rushing fit cannot make a lower-quality QB the top QB recommendation.
Live-sync safeguardsSleeper D/ST abbreviations now map to trainer names (for example HOU → Texans, DEN → Broncos, NE → Patriots). Known player aliases such as Kenny/Kenneth Gainwell are canonicalized, unmatched Sleeper picks are surfaced, and confirmed season-long exclusions are removed from Best Available.
Rank cleanup / identity safeguardsMichael Pittman Jr. is corrected to the current late-Round-9/10 value range, several established mid/late players now have embedded safety anchors, Sleeper rank matching is suffix-aware, and current injury watches can lower Champion Process scores without hard-excluding the player.

v6.8 preserves v6.7’s roster-completion deadlines, three draft modes, Champion Process, live Sleeper source-of-truth sync, time-aware ADP history, identity safeguards, simulation save/restore, bye/team context and final-turn logic. This update fixes the mid-round QB recommendation bug exposed in testing: when QB is empty from the 40/41 window onward, Champion Process now gates the QB lane through the current starter-quality order before overall Champion scoring. Only the top football-quality QB options can represent the position, and the best-quality available QB receives a small mid-round completion boost. Sleeper ADP/API rank remains timing information only; rushing/league fit remains a secondary separator rather than a way for a lower-quality QB to jump a clearly superior available starter.
